Four ways we help.

Each one is simple and practical, and each one is meant to stay in the community.

Motivation

Encouragement that keeps people going. Words, conversations and support for people who need a push to start or to keep on.

Art patronage

We support local artists by buying, sharing and showing their work, so talent in Bole gets seen and gets paid.

Value adding

Practical help for people to get more from what they already make or do, so their skills and products are worth more.

Digital skills for the young

Teaching young people how to use the internet safely, learn from it, and use it to build something for themselves.

Questions people ask.

Is this a charity?

It works for the community, but it is not a registered charity. It is an informal program run by one person. That is why we say it plainly, and why we cannot issue donation receipts.

Is my subscription a donation?

No. It is a payment for internet access. It also supports the program, since that is how the program funds itself.

What do I get when I subscribe?

Internet access for the number of days you pay for. Message us on WhatsApp to ask where and how it works.

How do I pay?

With mobile money. Choose your days, follow the steps in the form, then send us your payment details on WhatsApp.

Can I help in another way?

Yes. If you have art to share, a skill to teach or an idea for the program, message us on WhatsApp.
